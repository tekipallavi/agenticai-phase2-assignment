Based on the household profile and computed energy metrics, here are 4 practical energy-saving recommendations tailored to your house profile:

1. **Upgrade Lighting to LED Bulbs:**
   - Action: Replace all existing incandescent and CFL bulbs with LED bulbs.
   - Rationale: LED bulbs are more energy-efficient and have a longer lifespan. They consume significantly less electricity, leading to immediate energy savings on lighting.
   - Expected Benefit: LED bulbs can reduce lighting energy consumption by up to 75%, resulting in noticeable savings on your electricity bill.

2. **Optimize AC Usage and Set Thermostat Efficiently:**
   - Action: Program your thermostat to adjust the temperature when you are away or asleep. Consider increasing the temperature by a few degrees during the day.
   - Rationale: By reducing the workload on your AC units, you can lower energy consumption and improve efficiency.
   - Expected Benefit: Adjusting the thermostat by just 1 degree can save around 1-3% on cooling costs, resulting in reduced overall energy usage.

3. **Improve Insulation and Seal Windows:**
   - Action: Enhance insulation in your apartment, especially in attics, walls, and floors. Consider upgrading to double-pane windows for better insulation.
   - Rationale: Proper insulation helps maintain indoor temperatures, reducing the workload on heating and cooling systems.
   - Expected Benefit: Better insulation can lead to energy savings of up to 20% on heating and cooling costs, making your home more energy-efficient.

4. **Upgrade Water Heater to High-Efficiency Model:**
   - Action: Replace the current water heater with a high-efficiency model, preferably a heat pump water heater or a tankless water heater.
   - Rationale: Energy-efficient water heaters consume less electricity to heat water, resulting in lower energy usage and reduced utility bills.
   - Expected Benefit: Upgrading to a high-efficiency water heater can cut water heating costs by up to 50%, leading to significant energy savings over time.

Implementing these energy-saving recommendations can help you reduce your household's energy consumption and lower your utility bills while making your home more environmentally friendly.
